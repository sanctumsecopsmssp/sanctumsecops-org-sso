Verify a receipt

This verifier runs entirely in your browser. It recomputes the RFC 6962 Merkle path from the leaf digest up to the claimed root and compares. No request is made to the log operator, which is the whole point — a proof you have to ask us to confirm is not a proof.

Paste a receipt

JSON receipt body. The included sample is a genuine, self-consistent Merkle proof — the verifier below really does recompute it.

What this checks and what it does not. The Merkle recomputation is real and complete. Tree-head signature verification — the composite ECDSA P-256 plus ML-DSA-65 legs — is reported as not checked here, because ML-DSA is not available in WebCrypto and pulling a verifier into a static page would be worse than being honest about it. Full signature verification belongs in the CLI and the auditor portal.

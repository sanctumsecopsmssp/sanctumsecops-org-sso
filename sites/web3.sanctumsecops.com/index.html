Gate lab

Ten stage loaders for the chain surface

The corridor gate is architectural — a door, a vault, a perimeter — which suits an access surface. This one is not an access surface, it is a cryptographic one, so each candidate animates mathematics the site actually sells rather than decorating the wait. Where a loader can be driven by a real value from the build environment it is: the 2,376 alloc entries, the fork set at t=0 and the batch inbox below are the real ones.

10 candidates

Constraints Nothing here runs past 2.6 seconds. The existing gate takes about seven, which is right for a first-visit ceremony and wrong for a gate that fires on navigation. Gold appears at most once per loader, at the moment of resolution — it is a trust signal, not a colour. Every candidate has a reduced-motion end state: the resolved frame and the label, without the journey.

Curve Trace

01 · SIGNATURE VERIFIED
PRESS PLAY

secp256r1 draws itself, then a point walks it, each doubling leaving a decaying ghost until the public key lands in gold. Animates the exact curve a YubiKey signs on — the P256VERIFY precompile is the load-bearing fact of the whole chain.

2.10s · inline SVG

Merkle Ascent

02 · ROOT ANCHORED
PRESS PLAY

Leaf hashes pair off, hash into parents, and climb four levels to a single root that flares gold. A literal diagram of the product: the ledger publishes a signed tree head you can check yourself.

2.20s · CSS only

Genesis Crystallization

03 · GENESIS VERIFIED
PRESS PLAY

Particles rain in and lock into a hexagonal lattice, the count read from the real alloc entry total. The four forks tick gold in sequence, all at t=0. The most spectacular and the only one needing canvas.

2.50s · canvas + rAF

Hash Avalanche

04 · HASH SETTLED
PRESS PLAY

The wordmark churns into hex and settles into the real genesis hash left to right. Demonstrates the avalanche property in under a second — the only candidate cheap enough to fire on every internal navigation.

1.75s · CSS only

Keyspace Collapse

05 · KEY ESTABLISHED
PRESS PLAY

256 cells — the keyspace rendered as area rather than a number — eliminated in scattered order until one remains and turns gold. Reads as scale, which is the honest thing to convey about a 256-bit key.

2.00s · CSS only

Batch Settlement

06 · BATCH POSTED
PRESS PLAY

L2 transactions stream in, compress into a batch, and post to the batch inbox on L1, which flares once. The rollup’s real settlement path, using the real inbox address from the rollup config.

2.30s · CSS only

Lattice Reduction

07 · BASIS REDUCED
PRESS PLAY

A sheared lattice straightens while a long basis vector reduces to a short one in gold. The only candidate that speaks to the post-quantum half of the thesis — ML-KEM’s hardness is lattice hardness.

2.20s · inline SVG

Ring Alignment

08 · QUORUM SIGNED
PRESS PLAY

Four dashed rings spin at different rates until their gaps align and a solid gold ring closes through them. Signals multi-key authorisation: independent holders converging on one valid signature.

2.20s · inline SVG

SCN Mint

09 · SUPPLY MINTED
PRESS PLAY

The hexagon draws, fills with a gold gradient, and a balance counts up through all eight decimals — SCN’s actual precision. The most literally monetary of the ten.

2.10s · inline SVG

Entropy Drain

10 · ENTROPY CONSUMED
PRESS PLAY

Columns of bits flip and drain away, leaving one key behind. Conveys that a key is what remains after entropy is consumed rather than something summoned out of nothing.

2.05s · CSS only
